APPROACH AND PHILOSOPHY

My Creative Compass

Content, to me, is never just output. It's a conversation between a brand and a real human, and the second it feels manufactured, the conversation ends.

Whether I'm building a WhatsApp campaign flow, designing a reel hook, or writing an email sequence, I'm always asking: does this feel like something a person would actually stop for?

I believe the best creative work comes from understanding the audience so well that the content feels made just for them. That's the gap I obsess over: between generic content calendars and content that actually converts.

"data tells you what happened. empathy tells you why. good content needs both."

RESET

Ayurvedic Wellness Brand

Retention Marketing · WhatsApp + Email · Campaign Strategy

Channels: 2 - WhatsApp and Email

01

THE BRIEF

RESET sells Ayurvedic wellness products including ACV Gummies, Marine Collagen, Pain Relief, Detox Candy, and Vitamin C. My role was to build and execute a retention marketing system via WhatsApp and Email to reduce churn, re-engage lapsed customers, and drive repeat purchase.

02

THE CHALLENGE

  • Campaigns needed stronger lifecycle structure and product-wise segmentation.
  • Duplicate event triggers were creating Customer.io automation errors.
  • WhatsApp re-engagement needed more relevant cohort-based messaging.
03

WHAT I DID

  • Designed WhatsApp campaign flows using Customer.io.
  • Built 7 monthly campaign calendars with product-wise segmentation.
  • Debugged a duplicate Order Completed event in Customer.io.
  • Created HTML email creatives with UTM-tagged links for attribution.
04

IMPACT

Structured lifecycle workflows around behavioural segmentation and reactivation, contributing to stronger campaign efficiency and repeat-user journeys. Across managed lifecycle workflows, ROAS improved from 0.7–0.8x to a sustained 1.5–2x.

EvolvX

Health & Fitness Video-on-Demand Platform

Retention Campaigns · WhatsApp + Email · Content & Creative

Channels: 2 - WhatsApp and Email

01

THE BRAND

EvolvX is a popular video-on-demand health platform founded by fitness coach Puneet Rao, which integrates human coaching with digital tracking tools.

02

WHAT I BUILT

  • Built 6 monthly campaign calendars for lifecycle and retention communication.
  • Created WhatsApp campaigns using Interakt and email campaigns using Mailchimp.
  • Developed retention messaging for inactive, win-back, streak-broken, and renewal cohorts.
  • Created A/B copy variants and campaign creatives for testing.
03

MARKETING APPROACH

Segmented communication around where users were in their journey, making each message more relevant instead of treating every audience as one cohort.

04

KEY TAKEAWAY

Retention works when the message matches the user's moment. The work combined cohort-based communication, creative testing, and consistent WhatsApp + Email touchpoints to make lifecycle messaging more relevant.
