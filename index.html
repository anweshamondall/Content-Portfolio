APPROACH AND PHILOSOPHY

My Creative Compass

Content, to me, is never just output. It's a conversation between a brand and a real human, and the second it feels manufactured, the conversation ends.

Whether I'm building a WhatsApp campaign flow, designing a reel hook, or writing an email sequence, I'm always asking: does this feel like something a person would actually stop for?

I believe the best creative work comes from understanding the audience so well that the content feels made just for them. That's the gap I obsess over: between generic content calendars and content that actually converts.

"data tells you what happened. empathy tells you why. good content needs both."

Audience-First, Always

Every creative decision is reverse-engineered from who's watching, what they're feeling, and what would make them act.

Aesthetic + Analytical

Pretty doesn't cut it. I pair visual instinct with performance data — A/B tests, engagement rates, ROAS — because I want to know if the work moved the needle.

Retention Over Reach

Virality is a lottery. Retention is a system. I'm more interested in building content that makes someone return.

Ship, Learn, Iterate

I believe in getting things out, watching what happens, and evolving fast. Perfection is the enemy of progress, especially in social media.

CLIENT CASE STUDY

Client Case Study 01

RESET — Ayurvedic Wellness Brand

Retention Marketing · WhatsApp and Email Campaigns · Creative Design

Hired for: End-to-end retention lifecycle  |  Duration: Jan — May 2026

01

THE BRIEF

RESET sells Ayurvedic wellness products including ACV Gummies, Marine Collagen, Pain Relief, Detox Candy, and Vitamin C. My role was to build and execute a retention marketing system via WhatsApp and Email to reduce churn, re-engage lapsed customers, and drive repeat purchase.

02

CHALLENGES

  • No existing lifecycle flow — campaigns were ad hoc blasts
  • Duplicate event triggers causing Customer.io automation errors
  • Multiple product lines needing separate messaging strategies
  • Low re-engagement on WhatsApp with non-segmented audiences
03

MY APPROACH

  • Designed WhatsApp campaign flows using Interakt and Freshchat API
  • Built March and April campaign calendars with product-wise segmentation
  • Debugged duplicate Order Completed event in Customer.io
  • Created HTML email creatives with UTM-tagged links for attribution
  • Developed a complete handover doc with all campaign workflows
04

WHAT I LEARNED

Retention marketing is as much about timing and segmentation as it is about creative quality. A beautiful email sent to the wrong cohort at the wrong moment is invisible. Debugging automation logic requires both marketing intuition and technical patience — finding that duplicate event trigger was part detective work, part engineering.

OUTCOMES AND DELIVERABLES

0 Full campaign calendars built (March and April)
0 Product lines with individual messaging strategy
0 Customer.io automation error resolved
0 Full brand handover doc built for continuity
CLIENT CASE STUDY

Client Case Study 02

EvolvX — Indian OTT Fitness App

Creative Design · Campaign Copywriting · Persona-Based Content

Hired for: Creatives + WhatsApp retention cohorts  |  Duration: Mar — May 2026

01

THE BRIEF

EvolvX is a dark-themed fitness OTT with bold brand identity (#FF6200 accent, Bebas Neue typography). They needed retention creatives for user cohorts — inactive, winback, streak-broken, renewal — and social proof content across WhatsApp and email.

02

WHAT I BUILT

  • Six fitness persona avatar designs with distinct art styles
  • WhatsApp and email HTML creatives for each retention cohort
  • A/B copy variants for campaign testing
  • Dance workout ad creatives and social proof carousel content
  • Brand-consistent visual system across all touchpoints
03

CREATIVE DIRECTION

EvolvX's audience are young, aspirational fitness users. The challenge was striking a tone that felt motivational, not preachy — like a gym buddy texting you, not a brand broadcasting at you. Every cohort message was persona-specific, with different hooks for someone who'd broken their streak versus someone three days away from renewal.

04

KEY TAKEAWAY

Persona-based content is what separates a 5% open rate from a 22% one. When content speaks to someone's specific moment in their user journey, they feel seen. That's when clicks happen.
